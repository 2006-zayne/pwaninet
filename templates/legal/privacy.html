This policy explains what information Vasto uses, how it is handled, and how you can contact us about it.

Information collected

When you create an account, Vasto collects your username, password, programme, academic level, academic year, and semester. You may also provide your name in optional name fields.

You may provide profile details and content such as photos, posts, comments, group activity, study documents, feedback, and related interactions. A feedback submission includes its category, subject, message, the page path where it was submitted, and browser user-agent information. Vasto also records the account and date and time of authenticated activity, and handles account, session, device, and technical information needed to operate and protect the service.

When you use Spark, the AI assistant processes your chat messages, any files you upload, and contextual information about your academic profile (such as enrolled units and programme) to generate responses. Spark may send this information to third-party AI providers (currently Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter, depending on configuration) for processing. The specific providers used depend on the deployment configuration. Spark does not share your personal instructions or preference settings with other users.

Push notification subscriptions store a device token and browser identifier so notifications can be delivered. You can disable push notifications in your device or browser settings.

How information is used

Information is used to manage accounts, provide features, show content to the audiences selected for those features, review feedback and reports, understand platform activity, protect the service, respond to requests, and meet legal obligations.

Specifically, your academic profile (programme, level, semester, enrolled units) is used to personalise document recommendations, peer suggestions, and Spark responses. Your posts and documents are indexed for search according to your chosen visibility settings. Spark uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to search documents you are authorised to access and includes citations with its responses.

AI and embedding providers

Spark uses third-party AI providers for natural language responses and document embeddings. The configured providers include:

  • LLM providers: Google Gemini, Groq, and OpenRouter (used as fallback chain; only the configured and active provider processes your request).
  • Embedding providers: Google Gemini Embedding, Jina, Voyage AI, and Cloudflare (used to convert document chunks into vector embeddings for semantic search).

When you send a message to Spark, the content of that message and the retrieved context may be transmitted to the active LLM provider over HTTPS. These providers process the data solely to generate a response and do not use it to train models on your personal data, subject to their respective terms. The specific provider used depends on runtime configuration and fallback logic.

Document embeddings are generated from document chunks and stored in the Vasto database using pgvector. Embeddings are mathematical representations of text and do not contain readable copies of your documents.

Content you share

Your posts and uploads are associated with your account. Their visibility depends on feature settings and the audience you select. Share carefully and upload only material you created or have permission to share. Others may copy or save content they can access, so removing it from Vasto may not remove copies elsewhere.

You keep the rights you have in content you create. You allow Vasto to store and display that content as needed to provide the service and according to the audience settings you choose.

Service providers and retention

Service providers may process information on Vasto's behalf to host and operate the service. This includes database hosting, file storage (locally or via Cloudflare R2 with CDN delivery), email delivery, and push notification delivery. Vasto does not sell your personal information. Information is kept for as long as needed to provide and protect Vasto, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

You can request deletion of your content or account through available account settings. When you delete content, it is removed from active use; backups and logs may retain information for a limited period before being purged.

Cookies and device storage

Vasto uses cookies and browser or device storage to keep you signed in, remember settings, and support site features such as notifications and theme preferences. You can manage these through your browser or device settings; some features may not work properly if required storage is disabled.

Security

Vasto uses Django's built-in security features including CSRF protection, password hashing (PBKDF2), session management, and login rate-limiting (via django-axes). Two-factor authentication (TOTP) is available for your account. Uploaded files are stored separately from application code and are served through configured storage backends.

Your choices and requests

You can update information through available account settings. You can control who sees your profile, posts, and documents through per-item and default privacy settings. You can manage notification preferences, theme, language, and display options in Settings. You can ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
