# About Pwanimate

Pwanimate is the AI academic assistant built into PwaniNet. It helps students find academic resources, understand their campus structure, discover peers, and get answers to questions about their studies and the platform.

## What Pwanimate can do

- **Document search and analysis** — Searches the PwaniNet document repository (past papers, lecture notes, slides, and more) using semantic and keyword search. Provides citations with links to original documents.
- **Academic structure lookup** — Looks up information about faculties, schools, departments, programmes, and academic units (courses) at Pwani University.
- **Peer discovery** — Helps find other PwaniNet users who share your skills, interests, academic programme, or collaboration preferences. Respects privacy settings.
- **Group and announcement awareness** — Retrieves recent posts and announcements from groups you belong to.
- **Notification summary** — Shows a summary of your recent notifications.
- **Content search** — Searches posts and documents by text across the platform.
- **Web search** — Searches the public web for current events, recent research, or topics outside PwaniNet's knowledge base.
- **Personal reminders** — Creates in-app reminder notifications for you.
- **Study mode** — Dedicated Study Mode with step-by-step topic work, checkpoints, and progress tracking.
- **File attachments** — Processes uploaded files (images, PDFs, DOCX, PPTX, text) and uses them as context for responses.

## How Pwanimate works

Pwanimate uses a custom orchestration layer that combines intent detection, context engineering, tool calling, and AI generation:

1. **Intent detection** — Determines what you are asking about (casual conversation, general knowledge, document questions, PwaniNet questions, or personalised recommendations).
2. **Context engineering** — Assembles a bounded context package with relevant document chunks, your academic profile, and any tools it decides to call.
3. **Tool calling** — For queries requiring specific data (document metadata, peer profiles, notifications), calls deterministic tools that query the PwaniNet database directly.
4. **AI generation** — The final response is generated by a large language model from a configured provider (Gemini, Groq, or OpenRouter).
5. **Citations** — When Pwanimate uses information from specific documents, it includes citations that link back to the source.

## What Pwanimate does not do

- Does not access private messages, blocked users' content, or documents you are not authorised to view.
- Does not share your personal instructions, preference settings, or conversation history with other users.
- Does not make academic decisions, submit assignments, or communicate with faculty on your behalf.
- Does not proactively discuss PwaniNet as a platform unless you ask about it.
- Is an advisory tool and study companion, not a replacement for official university communication or faculty instruction.

## Data and privacy

- Your messages are sent to the configured AI provider for processing. The provider processes data solely to generate a response.
- Retrieved document chunks and site data are included in the AI provider's request context, from documents you are already authorised to access.
- Your conversation history is stored in the PwaniNet database and is private to your account.
- Your personal instructions and preference settings are never sent to other users or shared publicly.

## Customisation

You can customise Pwanimate's behaviour in Settings → Personalisation:
- **Tone** — Choose how formal or casual responses are.
- **Response style** — Control the level of detail in responses.
- **Personal instructions** — Add custom instructions that Pwanimate follows in every conversation.
