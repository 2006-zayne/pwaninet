# About vasto

vasto is a student community and learning-resource platform for Kenyan students. It brings student discussions, campus updates, groups, academic resources, and an AI assistant together in one place.

vasto is an independent project created by Zayne Earthman. vasto is not affiliated with any university or educational institution.

vasto was formerly known as Pwaninet. The service domain (pwaninet.app), app identifiers, and technical references may still use the previous name; they refer to the same service.

## Core Features

- **Posts and discussions** — Share text, images, videos, and links with your followers, everyone on vasto, or specific groups. Comment, react, and repost.
- **Groups** — Join course groups, departmental communities, or interest-based groups. Group admins can pin announcements and manage membership.
- **Academic documents** — Upload and browse past papers, lecture notes, slides, and other study materials organised by programme, academic unit, and semester.
- **Search** — Search across people, posts, documents, and groups in one place.
- **Spark** — An AI academic assistant built into vasto that can search documents, answer questions, and help discover peers. Spark was formerly known as Pwanimate.
- **Notifications** — In-app and push notifications for social, academic, and security events.
- **Recommendations** — Suggestions for classmates, groups, and documents based on your academic profile.

## Who can use vasto?

vasto is intended for students and learners. There is no age cutoff. You register with your programme, academic level, and semester so that content and recommendations are relevant to your studies.

## Mobile and offline

vasto works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) in your browser and has an Android app built with Capacitor. The PWA supports offline access to core pages and previously viewed content.
