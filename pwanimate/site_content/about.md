# About PwaniNet

PwaniNet is a student community and learning-resource platform for students at Pwani University. It brings student discussions, campus updates, groups, academic resources, and an AI assistant together in one place.

PwaniNet is an independent project created by Zayne Earthman, a student at Pwani University. It is not currently an official Pwani University service or a University assignment.

## Core Features

- **Posts and discussions** — Share text, images, videos, and links with your followers, everyone on PwaniNet, or specific groups. Comment, react, and repost.
- **Groups** — Join course groups, departmental communities, or interest-based groups. Group admins can pin announcements and manage membership.
- **Academic documents** — Upload and browse past papers, lecture notes, slides, and other study materials organised by programme, academic unit, and semester.
- **Search** — Search across people, posts, documents, and groups in one place.
- **Pwanimate** — An AI academic assistant built into PwaniNet that can search documents, answer questions, and help discover peers.
- **Notifications** — In-app and push notifications for social, academic, and security events.
- **Recommendations** — Suggestions for classmates, groups, and documents based on your academic profile.

## Who can use PwaniNet?

PwaniNet is intended for students enrolled at Pwani University. There is no age cutoff.

## Mobile and offline

PwaniNet works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) in your browser and has an Android app built with Capacitor. The PWA supports offline access to core pages and previously viewed content.
