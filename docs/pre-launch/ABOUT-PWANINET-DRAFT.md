# About PwaniNet

PwaniNet is a student community and learning-resource platform for students at Pwani University. It brings student discussions, campus updates, groups, and study resources together in one place, making it easier to discover useful information and connect with other students.

PwaniNet is an independent project created by Zayne Earthman, a student at Pwani University. It is not a University assignment or an official Pwani University service, and the University has not endorsed or approved it. “PwaniNet” is the name of this project and does not mean that Pwani University owns or operates it.

## Share thoughtfully

When you post or upload something, it is associated with your account and may be seen by other people according to the feature's audience and privacy settings. Think carefully before publishing: avoid sharing private or confidential information, and only upload photos, past papers, lecture notes, or other material that you have the right to share. Other people may copy or save content they can access, so deleting it from PwaniNet may not remove every copy.

## Who can use PwaniNet?

PwaniNet is intended for students enrolled at Pwani University. There is no age cutoff.
