# PwaniNet Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information PwaniNet collects when you use the site, how it is used, and how you can contact us about your information. For information about the project and its relationship to Pwani University, see [About PwaniNet](ABOUT-PWANINET-DRAFT.md).

## Information we collect

When you create an account, PwaniNet collects your username, password, programme, academic level, academic year, and semester. You may also provide your name in the optional name fields.

When you use PwaniNet, you may provide profile details and content such as photos, posts, comments, group activity, study documents, feedback, and related interactions. A feedback submission includes its category, subject, message, the page path where it was submitted, and your browser's user-agent information. For platform activity reporting, PwaniNet records your account and the date and time of authenticated activity. The site also handles account and device information needed to keep you signed in, protect accounts, and provide features you use. Basic technical information, such as browser and connection details, may be recorded to operate and secure the service.

## How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide PwaniNet features, show content and interactions to the audiences selected for those features, review feedback and reports, understand platform activity, protect the service, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations.

## Content you share

Your posts and uploads are associated with your account. Their visibility depends on the feature and its audience or privacy settings. Please think carefully before posting and avoid sharing private or confidential information. Only upload photos, past papers, lecture notes, and other material that you created or have permission to share. Other people may copy or save content they can access, so removing content from PwaniNet may not remove copies elsewhere.

You keep the rights you have in content you create. You allow PwaniNet to store and show the content as needed to provide the service and according to the audience settings you choose.

## Service providers

PwaniNet may use service providers to host and operate the site and its features. Those providers may process information on PwaniNet's behalf to provide their services. PwaniNet does not sell your personal information.

## How long information is kept

We keep information only for as long as it is needed to provide and protect PwaniNet, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

## Your choices and requests

You can update information through the account settings and controls available on PwaniNet. You can contact us to ask about your personal information, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

## Cookies and device storage

PwaniNet uses cookies and browser or device storage to keep you signed in, remember settings, and support site features such as notifications. You can manage cookies through your browser or device settings; some site features may not work properly if required storage is disabled.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the latest version will be made available on PwaniNet with its effective date. Please review it when you use the service.
